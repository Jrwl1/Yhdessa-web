## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Yhdessä is a speech therapy practice website. Visitors should understand the services, learn about the therapist and find the contact route.

## Users
Children, young people and adults, and their close networks. Give all ages equal weight in the redesign, as proposed and approved for implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React/Vite/Tailwind app with Home, About and Contact routes in Finnish, Swedish and English. Preserve content and behavior. Contact delivery is not connected; do not imply successful sending. Therapist biography and contact details remain incomplete. Admin behavior is outside this redesign.

## Brand Commitments
Use the supplied yhdessa_grafisk_manual.pdf as visual reference. Preserve the authentic supplied logo. Generate supporting still-life imagery only; never generate the therapist portrait. The user approved direct implementation of the proposed warm, geometric, restrained direction.
