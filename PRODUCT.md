## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Yhdessä is a speech therapy practice website. Visitors should understand the services, learn about the therapist and find the contact route.

## Users
Children and young people, particularly children receiving Kela intensive medical rehabilitation, and their close networks in the Turku region. Adults are also welcome.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React/Vite/Tailwind app with Home, About and Contact routes in Finnish, Swedish and English. Therapy is offered in Finnish and Swedish; guidance for the support network is also offered in English. The customer supplied biography and contact details on September 21, 2026. Contact uses direct email and phone links instead of a disconnected form. Admin behavior is outside this redesign.

## Brand Commitments
Use the supplied yhdessa_grafisk_manual.pdf as visual reference. Preserve the authentic supplied logo. Generate supporting still-life imagery only; never generate the therapist portrait. The user approved the pink-background/orange-logo direction, reversed in the footer, on September 21, 2026.
